How to play

Getting started

  • The host creates a room and shares the link or the 4-letter code.
  • Players open the link on their own screen and enter a name.
  • The host spins to choose who picks first. On a replay, last game's Golden Prism winner picks first.

The board

  • 5 categories, each with clues worth 100 to 500. Bigger values are harder.
  • The player in control picks the next clue. Answer correctly to take control.

Buzzing in

  • The host reads the clue. Buzzers open a few seconds later, longer for longer questions.
  • Whoever picked the clue gets a 6-second head start. If they don't buzz in time, they're out on that clue and it opens to everyone else.
  • First to buzz answers out loud, and the host decides if it's right. Tap the buzzer or press Space.

Scoring

  • Right: you earn the clue's value and pick next.
  • Wrong: you lose the clue's value (unless the host turns that off), you're out for that clue, and the others can steal. The answer stays hidden until someone gets it or everyone's out.

Daily Double

  • One clue worth 300 or more hides a Daily Double, with a harder question than usual.
  • Only the player who found it answers. They wager at least 5, and up to their score or 1,000, whichever is higher.
  • The host reveals the clue after the wager. Right or wrong, you win or lose what you wagered. No steals.

Final Showdown

  • When the board is empty, everyone sees the final category and wagers 0 up to their score.
  • Then the question appears, and you have 30 seconds to type your answer.
  • The host reveals each answer, lowest score first. Right adds your wager, wrong takes it away.

Tiebreaker

  • If first place is tied, only the tied players buzz on a harder question, with 15 seconds to buzz.
  • The first correct answer wins. If nobody gets it, another question comes up.
  • The winner takes home the Golden Prism.
